# John's reply to Email 1 (2026-11-19)

Hi Nneka,

Great questions. Yes, your understanding is right: most candidates we lose are good fits who fall through the gaps between steps.

1. Most of our recruiters' time goes to chasing people: texting and calling candidates to get them to their next step, and checking inboxes for results. If we could fix one thing first, it would be follow-ups and knowing where every candidate is. Right now nobody has a clear picture.

2. Most candidates find us through our career page where they apply online through our application form. A recruiter usually reaches out within a few days, sometimes a week. After that, everything happens through texts and calls from the recruiter's work phone: what to do next, where to go, what to bring. Nothing is automated, so if a recruiter is busy, the candidate hears nothing.

3. How each step works today:
- Background check and driving record: the recruiter orders it through our background check vendor's website. Results come back by email in 3 to 5 days.
- DOT drug test: the recruiter emails the candidate an authorization form, and the candidate goes to a clinic in our vendor's network. Results come back 2 to 5 days after the test. Our drug test vendor offers an integration, so results can come into another system automatically.
- CDL permit: the candidate books their own DMV appointment and takes the knowledge test. We verify the permit at the very end, before the offer.
- Training: paid training over Zoom.
- Skills test: done on an online assessment platform, which also offers an integration, so results can come in automatically.

4. About 300 applicants a month across 12 depots, all in one state. We hire around 17 drivers a month and need about 35. Peak hiring is July and August, before the school year.

5. Screening usually takes 1 to 2 weeks, because our first response is slow and some candidates take another job before we reach them. The background check takes 3 to 4 weeks. Results come back in 3 to 5 days once the candidate has done their part, but getting them to fill out the vendor's form is the holdup, and recruiters don't always follow up when they don't. The drug test also takes 3 to 4 weeks, and it's where we lose the most people: candidates put off going to the clinic or miss their appointment, and it takes reminder after reminder to get them in. Once someone gets to training on Zoom, things move quickly. Training takes about a week, and the skills test takes a few days. The last 1 to 2 weeks are mostly spent waiting on candidates who haven't gotten their permit from the DMV yet, before we can verify it and send the offer. So most of our 90 days goes to the background check and drug test, waiting on candidates to actually go do them.

6. Yes, strictly in order: screening, background check, drug test, CDL permit, training, skills test, offer. We don't want to pay for drug tests or training for someone who won't clear the earlier steps.

7. We want a recruiter to review before anyone is rejected, including failed drug tests and screening requirements. Offers are different: once someone clears every step, the offer can go out automatically.
